\begin{highlights}
    \item Time-window penalties from earliest-arrival bounds, with no extra qubits or gates.
    \item Provably sound: no feasible (on-time) route is ever penalised.
    \item In exact QAOA simulation, E1+C raises P(opt) over a window-free encoding.
    \item Beats slack-qubit and unbalanced-penalization QUBOs under simulated annealing.
    \item Prunes most late routes on instances with up to nine customers.
\end{highlights}
